🦄 Do Unicorns Exist? — Website README
📘 Overview

Do Unicorns Exist? is a simple, informative, and visually appealing HTML website that explores the age-old question of whether unicorns are real.
It combines mythical storytelling with scientific facts about a real prehistoric creature — Elasmotherium sibiricum, also known as the Siberian Unicorn.

The goal of the website is to help readers understand how myths can be inspired by real creatures and to showcase the beauty of combining science and imagination.

🧩 Features

🌈 Magical Unicorn Theme:
A stunning fantasy background with glowing text and elegant colors.

🦏 Real Science Section:
Detailed information on Elasmotherium sibiricum, including its size, horn, and habitat.

📸 High-Quality Images:
Includes realistic illustrations and fossil photographs with captions.

💬 Informative Text:
Simple, readable explanations designed for students or anyone curious about myths and prehistoric life.

✨ Responsive Layout:
Looks great on desktop and tablet screens.

🖼️ Content Summary

Introduction:
Talks about unicorn myths from different cultures.

The Real “Unicorn” – Elasmotherium sibiricum:
Explains the prehistoric rhinoceros that might have inspired unicorn legends.

Images and Captions:
Includes three visuals: a mythical unicorn, an artist’s impression of Elasmotherium, and its fossil skull.

Conclusion:
Wraps up by showing how legends often have roots in real history.
